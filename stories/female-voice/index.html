Sleep stories read in a female voice.

3 stories · endings given away

Which voice puts you under is not a preference anyone can argue you out of, and it is one of the few things about sleep audio worth choosing deliberately. Some listeners settle faster to a higher register; others find it carries too much brightness into a dark room.

These are the stories read by Emma from Oxford, Amy from Greenwich and Niamh from Kinsale. What they have in common is not pitch but pacing: no performance, no characters, no leaning on a word to tell you it matters. The delivery flattens rather than dramatises, and the last third of every recording is quieter and slower than the first, on purpose, whether or not you are still awake to notice.

If none of them work, the same catalogue read in a male voice is one page over, and every one of them is written out in full to read if you would rather not listen at all. There are no ads and no music in any of them.

  • ▶ 40 minCozy Tales · Niamh from Kinsale
    The Midnight Sleeper Train Across the Alps

    Board the midnight-blue sleeper in Vienna, hand over your passport, and let the train do the rest while borders pass over you like weather.

  • ▶ 41 minCosmic Journeys · Amy from Greenwich
    Floating Through the Pillars of Creation

    You rise from your own roof and keep going, past every planet, until the stars thicken into a cloud with three dark columns standing in the middle of it.

  • ▶ 40 minAncient Worlds · Emma from Oxford
    Aristotle, the Greatest Philosopher

    A boy on a Greek shore who never stopped asking what is this, and the twenty-three centuries of patient looking that followed.
