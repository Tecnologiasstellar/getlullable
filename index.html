For overthinkers

Your brain isn't broken — it's just curious.

It doesn't need emptying. An unoccupied mind goes looking for work, and at midnight the only work available is tomorrow. So we give it something peaceful to ponder — with the ending given away in the first line, so there is never a reason to stay awake for it.

The research

Occupying a mind is not the same as emptying one.

Poor sleepers' minds at bedtime run planning and problem-solving; good sleepers drift through loose, neutral imagery. That difference is the whole design brief. A story interesting enough to hold attention and flat enough to release it moves you from the first state to the second — then keeps going without you.

  1. Racing thoughts delay sleep onset; the kind of thought predicts it better than the amount. Lemyre et al., Sleep Medicine Reviews, 2020
  2. Serially imagining unrelated, emotionally flat things lowered pre-sleep arousal and sleep effort in randomised work on the “cognitive shuffle.” Beaudoin, Simon Fraser University
  3. Ten to twenty minutes to fall asleep is the healthy range. Falling asleep instantly usually signals sleep debt, not success. Ohayon et al., Sleep Health, 2017

This is the research on the mechanism, not on Lullable. No sleep app can honestly claim clinical proof of itself, and ours does not. Judge the evidence yourself; then judge the recording with your own ears.
