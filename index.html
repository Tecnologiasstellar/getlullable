The research

The mechanism is studied.

Racing thoughts delay sleep — content displaces them.

Poor sleepers' bedtime minds run planning and problem-solving; good sleepers drift through loose, neutral imagery. Occupying the mind changes which one you get.

Lemyre et al., Sleep Medicine Reviews, 2020 →

Random, neutral imagery eases the descent.

In randomized research on the "cognitive shuffle," serially imagining unrelated, emotionally flat things lowered pre-sleep arousal and sleep effort. Our stories run the shuffle for you.

Beaudoin, Simon Fraser University →

10–20 minutes to fall asleep is healthy.

Sleep-quality research treats a gentle descent as normal — falling asleep instantly usually signals sleep debt, not success. The goal is a calm descent, not a stopwatch.

Ohayon et al., Sleep Health, 2017 →

No sleep app can honestly claim clinical proof of its own — including ours. This is the research on the mechanism; judge it yourself.
