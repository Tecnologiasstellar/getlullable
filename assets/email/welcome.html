What Lullable is

A free iPhone app that reads you a true story at bedtime — Roman bathhouses, the deep sea, the rings of Saturn — softly enough that you fall asleep partway through.

How it gets you to sleep

1 It occupies your mind instead of emptying it.
White noise is too empty to quiet a busy head. Podcasts are too interesting. This sits in the gap.
2 Every story gives away its ending in the first minute.
So there is nothing left to stay awake for. No cliffhanger, no next episode.
3 It fades to silence on its own.
Nothing to switch off, and nothing to wake you at 3 am.

In the morning: no sleep score, no streak, no grade. Only the sentence you disappeared on.

Download Lullable — free

On the App Store. No ads, no account, nothing to cancel.

 

What turns up on Sundays

One letter, Sunday evening: three quiet paragraphs about something genuinely fascinating. No news, nothing to act on, no quiz. Past subjects, so you know the register:

The patience of Roman concrete
The weather at the bottom of the sea
Two men on Ben Nevis, every hour, for 21 years

The library, meanwhile, is already waiting. A new story every night.

 

One letter a week, never two. Nothing after 9 pm, ever.
