Lullable is an iPhone app where a calm voice reads you fascinating true things — the slow life of a redwood, a Roman bathhouse at closing time, the weather at the bottom of the sea — so unhurriedly that you are not meant to reach the end. No music, no breathing exercises, nothing to do. If you already talk to an audience at bedtime, we would like you to tell them about it, and we would like to pay you properly when they download it.

## The offer

- **$0.50 for every first-time iPhone download** the App Store credits to your link.
- **One link**, yours: getlullable.com/go/yourcode. Say it on air, print it in the notes, put it in your bio.
- **Paid monthly**, by PayPal or Wise. No network to join, no dashboard to learn, no exclusivity.

## Who this is for

- Podcasters, especially anything people put on to wind down — history, science, slow fiction, ASMR, "boring" on purpose.
- YouTube, TikTok and Instagram creators in sleep, overthinking, ADHD, parenting, and the pleasantly uneventful corners of the internet.
- Newsletter writers with a bedtime-shaped audience.
- Not for coupon or deal sites, paid search, or anyone who would need to promise a result to make the sale. We never promise results, and neither can you.

## How it works

- **1. Email us.** Tell us where you publish (links), roughly how many people listen or watch, and the code you would like — usually your show or handle, lowercase, letters only. We reply within a week; every creator is approved by a person.
- **2. You get your link.** getlullable.com/go/yourcode. It is the only thing your audience needs to remember.
- **3. You get paid.** Every first-time download Apple attributes to your link earns $0.50. You get a statement on the first of each month and the money thirty days later.

## What we pay, exactly

- **$0.50 per first-time download** that App Store Connect attributes to your code. Re-downloads, your own installs, and anything Apple flags do not count.
- **Apple's window is 24 hours:** a download counts if it happens within a day of the tap on your link, and the most recent link wins. That is Apple's rule, not ours, and it is the same for every creator.
- **Pilot terms:** the first twenty creators, up to 500 paid downloads per creator per month. Both numbers go up once the first statements are out; we are proving the mechanism before we scale it.
- **Statement on the 1st** for the previous month; **paid thirty days later** by PayPal or Wise, your choice. Anything under $25 rolls into the next month.
- **The number comes from Apple's own campaign report,** which shows first-time downloads per link. It is the same figure we both see. There is no cookie, no pixel, and nothing installed in the app to make this work — which is also why it cannot be gamed by either of us.

## Before the app launches

The app is not in the App Store yet. Your link works today: it sends people to the waitlist, tagged with your code, and we will tell you how many signed up. We do not pay for signups. On launch day the same link starts sending iPhones straight to the App Store with your tag attached, and everyone you sent to the waitlist gets the launch email carrying your code — so the audience you build now is credited to you when the downloads happen. Nothing about your link changes.

- Nothing about treating, curing or fixing anything. Lullable is not a medical device, and a story is not care.
- No promised results — nothing about faster, deeper or better sleep, no before-and-after, no numbers. We describe what it does, never what it will do for someone.
- Nothing "proven", nothing "recommended by" anyone.
- Not the price. It is not announced yet. "There's a free tier" is the whole sentence.
- No paid ads on the word Lullable, no coupon sites, no incentives for people to download.

**Say that we pay you, out loud and up front.** The line is: *"Lullable pays me a commission if you download through my link."* In the audio or on screen at the start of the mention, not only in the description. This is the law in the US, the UK, the EU and Mexico, and it is also just honest — the reason your audience trusts you is the reason we want to work with you.

## Questions people ask before applying

### How do you know a download came from me?

Your link carries your code. Before launch it lands people on our site with the code attached, and the code is saved if they join the waitlist. After launch it sends iPhones to the App Store with the code as Apple's campaign tag, and App Store Connect counts first-time downloads per tag. We read that report once a month and pay from it.

### Is there a cookie?

No. The code travels in the link and nowhere else. Nothing is stored on anyone's device, which is why the window is Apple's 24 hours rather than the thirty or sixty days you may have seen elsewhere.

### What if someone hears the episode and downloads a week later?

Then Apple cannot connect it to you, and we cannot pay for it. We know that undercounts you. It is also why we pay per download rather than per subscriber, and why the rate is what it is.

### Android?

Not yet. iPhone only. Android listeners who tap your link land on our site and can join the newsletter.

### Can I offer a discount code?

Not yet. When there is a paid tier and enough volume, top partners will get an App Store offer code to say on air. Ask.

### Can I run ads for my link?

Not on the word Lullable, and not on coupon or deal sites. Your own channels, your own audience, your own words.

### When exactly do I get paid?

Statement on the first of the month for the previous month, payment thirty days later, PayPal or Wise, once the balance is $25 or more.
