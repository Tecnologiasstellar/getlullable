## The short version

We collect one thing directly: your email address, and only if you hand it over.

Beyond that, the site uses analytics and advertising cookies — the ordinary machinery that tells us which page brought you here and whether an ad was worth paying for. **None of it runs until you say yes.** Decline and you get the entire site anyway: every essay, every story, the audio sample, the newsletter. Nothing is withheld and nothing is degraded. We think that's the only version of consent that means anything.

We don't sell your data for money. California law counts advertising cookies as "sharing" even so, and you can switch that off in one click.

If you want out entirely, write to [info@getlullable.com](mailto:info@getlullable.com) and we'll delete what we hold. You don't need a reason and we won't ask for one.

Everything below is the same promise, said carefully, because some of it has to be.

## What we collect

**If you join the waitlist or the newsletter.** Your email address, and the date you gave it to us. That's the whole record. It is stored with Buttondown, our newsletter provider. Every email we send has a one-click unsubscribe link that works without contacting anybody.

**When you visit the site.** Our host, Vercel, keeps standard server logs — IP address, browser and device type, the page requested, the time — for security and to keep the site up. Those logs are necessary to serve you a page at all, so they aren't optional, and we don't read them for marketing.

**Fonts.** The site loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

**If you accept cookies.** Analytics and advertising tools then collect usage and device data — pages viewed, time on page, approximate location from your IP, referring site, device and browser type, and advertising identifiers assigned by the ad platforms. The next section names every one of them.

**What we never collect.** No health data — we do not ask how you slept, and Lullable is not a sleep tracker. No precise location. No contacts, photos, or microphone access. No sensitive data of any kind as Mexican and European law define it.

**The app, when it exists.** The Lullable app has not launched. When it does, this section will describe exactly what it collects — expected to be an email address for your account, your listening progress so playback resumes where you left off, and your subscription status. Payments will be handled by Apple or Google; we will never see your card. We will update this page before the app ships, not after, and the App Store privacy label will match this text.

## Cookies, and how to refuse them

A banner asks you on your first visit. Until you answer it, nothing in the optional categories loads — no script is fetched, no identifier is set, nothing is sent anywhere. The single exception is the record of your answer itself, which we store on your device so we don't have to ask again; that one is unavoidable and carries no identifier.

**Strictly necessary — always on.** Your cookie choice, and the security and load-balancing that our host applies to every request. Without these the site cannot be delivered, so there is nothing to consent to.

**Analytics — only with your consent.** Google Analytics 4, and a cookieless analytics tool that counts visits without storing anything on your device. These tell us which essays people read and where readers arrive from.

**Advertising — only with your consent.** Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, and Google Ads measurement tags. These tell an ad platform that a visit or a signup followed one of its ads, and allow it to show Lullable to you again elsewhere.

**How to change your mind, at any time:**

- Reopen the banner from the link in the site footer and choose again — a refusal takes effect immediately and removes what was set.
- Turn on **Global Privacy Control** in your browser or extension. We treat a GPC signal as a valid refusal of analytics and advertising cookies, and as a California opt-out of sharing, automatically and without you doing anything else.
- Clear cookies in your browser settings, or block them there entirely.
- Or email [info@getlullable.com](mailto:info@getlullable.com) and we'll handle it.

Refusing costs you nothing. There is no paywall, no nag, no reduced version of the site.

## Why we use it, and what's optional

**Necessary purposes** — without these there is no service to speak of:

- To send you the newsletter and the launch announcement you asked for.
- To keep the site running, fast, and free of abuse.
- To answer you when you write to us.
- To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

**Optional purposes** — you can refuse these and lose nothing:

- To measure which pages and episodes people actually finish.
- To measure whether our advertising works, and to show Lullable again to people who visited.
- To ask you for feedback, or to invite you to test something early.

Refuse the optional ones through the cookie banner, by unsubscribing, or by writing to [info@getlullable.com](mailto:info@getlullable.com). Saying no does not affect anything in the first list.

**Legal bases (GDPR).** Consent for the newsletter, for analytics and advertising cookies, and for the optional purposes — withdrawable at any moment, with no effect on what was lawful before you withdrew. Legitimate interest for site security and basic operation. Legal obligation for records we're required to keep.

We use a small number of providers, each doing one job, each bound to process data only as agreed:

- **Vercel** — website hosting and server logs (United States).
- **Buttondown** — newsletter delivery and subscriber list (United States).
- **Google** — Fonts on every page; Analytics 4 and Google Ads measurement only with your consent (United States and elsewhere).
- **Meta and TikTok** — advertising measurement and retargeting, only with your consent (United States and elsewhere).
- **Our cookieless analytics provider** — aggregate visit counts, with no cookie and no personal identifier.

That is the complete list. We do not sell personal data for money, and we have no interest in doing so. We disclose data to authorities only where a valid legal order requires it.

**A word about the advertising platforms.** When you consent to advertising cookies, Meta, TikTok, and Google receive data about your visit and use it for their own purposes too, under their own policies, which we don't control. That is the honest trade of running ads at all, and it is exactly what the banner is asking you about.

**International transfers.** We are a Mexican company and our providers are largely in the United States, so your data is stored and processed outside Mexico and outside the European Economic Area. Transfers rely on your consent and on the standard contractual protections our providers offer. If you'd rather your data not be transferred this way, decline the banner and don't subscribe — and if something is already there, write to us and we'll remove it.

## How long we keep it

Your email stays on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and it is removed within 30 days of either. Server logs roll off within 30 days at our host's default. Analytics data is kept for no more than 14 months. Data held by the advertising platforms follows their own retention periods, which are described in their policies and are typically shorter. Records we're legally required to retain — invoices, tax documents — are kept for the period Mexican law demands and no longer.

## Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. One email does all of it: [info@getlullable.com](mailto:info@getlullable.com). We answer within 20 business days, which is the Mexican legal deadline and the tightest one that applies to us. There is no charge, and we never make you create an account to exercise a right.

**In Mexico (LFPDPPP).** You hold ARCO rights — Acceso, Rectificación, Cancelación, Oposición — plus the right to revoke consent and to limit the use or disclosure of your data; the cookie banner is one of the means we offer for that. To exercise them, email us with your name, a way to reach you, proof of identity, and a clear description of what you want. If you're unhappy with our answer, you may go to the competent Mexican data protection authority.

**In the EU or UK (GDPR).** Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent at any time. You may also complain to your national supervisory authority.

**In California (CCPA/CPRA).** You have the right to know, delete, and correct, and the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. To be precise about a word that gets misused: **we do not sell personal information for money**, but running Meta, TikTok, and Google advertising tags counts as "sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising" under California law. You can opt out of it by declining or withdrawing consent in the cookie banner, or by sending a Global Privacy Control signal, which we honour automatically. We do not knowingly share the personal information of anyone under 16. We will never discriminate against you for exercising a right, and we extend these controls to every visitor regardless of whether California's thresholds apply to us.

## Children

Lullable is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13 — or under 16 in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. We do not knowingly collect data from them, and we do not target advertising at them. If a child's data reached us, write to [info@getlullable.com](mailto:info@getlullable.com) and we'll delete it promptly. Parents are of course welcome to play a Lullable story to a child; that requires no account and gives us nothing.

## Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Access to the subscriber list is limited to the people who need it, protected by strong authentication. We keep very little ourselves, which is the most reliable security measure available to us. No system is perfect, and if a breach ever affected your data we would tell you and the relevant authority without delay.

## A note on health

Lullable is audio, not medicine. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. We do not collect health data of any kind, and we never build advertising audiences around anything health-related. If you're struggling with sleep, please talk to a doctor — see the [Terms](/terms/) for the fuller version of this.
