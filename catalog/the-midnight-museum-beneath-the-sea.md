Red leaves the water first, absorbed in the first bright fathoms. Then orange. Then yellow. By fifty metres the world is blue and only blue, and by two hundred the blue itself is a memory of a glow far overhead, like a skylight in a vast dim gallery.

The ending, given away now: nothing is hunted and nothing is found. Nine of every ten creatures down there make their own cold light, they go on making it, and the glass sponges filter the same gentle current they have filtered for a very long time.

## The first minute

> The first hall of the museum is the light itself, and the museum arranges it in floors. Near the surface, the water is all sun and motion, gold and green, busy as a foyer. But sink a little, and the colours begin to close their doors. Red leaves first, absorbed in the first bright fathoms. Then orange. Then yellow. By fifty metres the world is blue, only blue, a deep cathedral blue that gets deeper the further you fall. By two hundred metres, the blue itself is only a memory of a glow, far overhead, like a skylight in a vast dim gallery…

## Why this one works at night

It is the shortest story in the catalogue, and the only one built around a single image held for twenty minutes: a dark room that lights itself. There is no plot to lose your place in, and losing your place is what the whole thing is for.
